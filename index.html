Nepali migrant workers and the city

Research on housing conditions, spatial inequality, and the lived experiences of Nepali migrant workers in Dubai and Doha.

Read the research overview

This ongoing thesis examines accommodation conditions, where worker housing is located, and how those environments shape everyday life and dignity. The planned approach combines oral histories with returnee workers, institutional perspectives, and literature and policy analysis.
